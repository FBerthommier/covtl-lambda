Data: the import system
------------------------

Everything audible or visible in `demos/` was **synthesized locally**
by the covtl-lambda contributors with the patched engine:

1. **Source of the stimuli** — engine SAMPA words synthesized by
   covtl-pipeline (`vtl-synth` subcommands) with the default jd3
   speaker and gesture timings (100/80/200 ms); no recordings of
   human speakers are used anywhere in this repository.
2. **Import into the demos** — the generators
   (`examples/html/make_lambda_demo.py`, `examples/html/make_pouw_demos.py`)
   extract, per λ step: the AAC-encoded audio, the tract-parameter
   figures, and the polar trajectories rebuilt from the engine's own
   `.polar` builder — and embed them as base64 payloads into a single
   self-contained HTML page. At run time a demo has **no dependency**
   on the engine, on the network, or on any external file; it is a
   pure display of previously synthesized data.
3. **Consequence for licensing** — since the payloads are outputs of
   a GPL-3.0 engine driven by GPL-3.0-or-later generator code, the
   embedded data is distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later together with
   the pages that carry it.
4. **Third-party research data** — none is redistributed. The Pouw
   et al. (2024) experiment is *cited* as the design reference of the
   replication frames (sentence pairs P1/P2/P4, NV1/NV3/NV4); every
   stimulus in `demos/pouw/` is a fresh synthesis, not a copy of
   theirs.
